Important Steps for Importing into Tally

Step 1: Take a Full Company Backup First
Always create a backup of your destination company (TBK file) before importing any external XML files.
Step 2: Configure Voucher Numbering Method
In Tally, navigate to Alter → Voucher Type for the relevant vouchers (Sales, Purchase, etc.). Set Method of Voucher Numbering to Automatic (Manual Override) or Manual to prevent voucher numbers from shifting.
Step 3: Import Cleaned Transactions
Go to Import → Transactions in Tally, select your cleaned Cleaned_DayBook.xml file, and import. Your new transactions will be added smoothly without overwriting existing data.
